Frequently Asked Questions

Is the AI theologically sound?

Yes. ChooseGOD uses theological guardrails to ensure all AI responses align with orthodox Christian doctrine. Pastors can also customize guidance for their congregation's denominational context.

Do my members need to pay for the app?

Members can use the free tier of ChooseGOD. Your church partnership unlocks enhanced features for your congregation. Members who want premium personal features can upgrade individually.

Can I create sermon follow-up plans?

Absolutely. After each sermon, create a guided study plan with Scripture references, reflection questions, and AI-powered discussion prompts that your members can work through during the week.

What analytics do I get?

See congregation-wide engagement: daily active readers, chapters read, prayer activity, devotional completion rates, and more. Growth and Church tiers include detailed reports and exports.

Can I try it before committing?

The Free tier is completely free with no time limit — perfect for trying ChooseGOD with your leadership team or a small group before rolling it out to your full congregation.

How do I invite my congregation?

Share a unique invite link or QR code. Members download the app, enter your church code, and they're connected. It takes less than 2 minutes.
